Connector guide

Use Jock Taxes inside Claude, ChatGPT, and Grok

The Jock Taxes connector lets your AI assistant run the Jock Taxes calculators for you. Ask in plain English for after-tax pay, the salary that matches another team, or the effect of a trade. NHL, NFL, MLB, and NBA are included.

What you can ask

After-tax pay

Estimate federal, state, provincial, local, and jock tax on one season's pay for a player on any team.

Equivalent salary

Find the salary on another team that leaves a player with the same after-tax income.

Player movement

See how after-tax pay changes when a player moves teams at the same salary.

You don't need to know the tool names. Start a chat with "Use JockTaxes…" and describe what you want. The assistant picks the right tool, looks up team codes and seasons, and asks you for anything it still needs.

Add the connector

Pick your assistant. Each one takes about a minute.

Claude

  1. Create your free Jock Taxes account (strongly suggested).
  2. Open Claude's add-connector dialog: add JockTaxes to Claude.
  3. The name and URL are filled in for you. Claude warns that the connector came from an external link. Check that you see:
    Connector name: JockTaxes
    Connector URL: https://jocktaxes.com/mcp/claude
    Then click Continue.
  4. For Authentication, choose Sign in now and sign in with your Jock Taxes email and password. To try it without an account, choose Sign in when needed instead. Don't choose No sign-in, even if Claude marks it as Detected, because Claude can't ask you to sign in later with it. Leave the other fields as they are.
  5. On the permissions screen, choose Always allow if you don't want to approve every calculation.

ChatGPT

  1. Optional but recommended: create your free Jock Taxes account.
  2. Open chatgpt.com/plugins.
  3. Open the Add dropdown and choose Add custom MCP server.
  4. Set the name to Jock Taxes and paste the server URL:
    https://jocktaxes.com/mcp/chatgpt
  5. Under Authentication, choose OAuth.
  6. Read the risk warning, select I understand and want to continue, then Create as a plugin. If a Jock Taxes sign-in opens, sign in with your account.
  7. Open your personal plugins, select Jock Taxes, and install it.
  8. In a new chat, type @ and pick Jock Taxes, or start with "Use JockTaxes…".

Don't see "Add custom MCP server"? It isn't on every ChatGPT plan. On a Business, Enterprise, or Edu workspace, an admin may need to turn it on first.

Grok

  1. Create your free Jock Taxes account (required for Grok).
  2. Open grok.com/connectors.
  3. Choose New Connector, then Custom.
  4. Name it JockTaxes and paste the connector URL:
    https://jocktaxes.com/mcp/grok
  5. When Grok asks you to grant access, sign in with your Jock Taxes email and password, then allow access.
  6. In a new chat, start with "Use JockTaxes…".

On a Grok Business or Enterprise team, an admin adds the connector URL in the team connector settings first. You then connect it on grok.com.

The connector gives your assistant these tools. There is one calculator, one salary matcher, and one movement tool per league, plus helpers that look up teams and players.

Details you can add

Only a team and a salary are needed. Mention any of these in your request to make the estimate closer to a real situation. Anything you leave out uses the default.

DetailWhat to sayDefault
Filing status"single" or "married filing jointly"Single
Season"for the 2025-26 season"Latest season for that league
Home (domicile)"lives in Florida year-round"The team's home state or province
Playoff games"assume 12 playoff games"None
Birth country"Canadian-born player"Not set
Bonus split"$4M base, $2M signing bonus, $500K performance bonus"Whole salary taxed as game-day pay
Take-home"include payroll taxes, a 3% agent fee, and a max 401(k)"Income tax only
Canadian retirement"with an RRSP contribution" or "with an RCA contribution" (Canadian teams)None

How bonuses are taxed: by default, a signing bonus in a split is taxed only where the player lives, not in each road-game state. A performance bonus is added to jock tax only if you ask. NFL requests also accept other game-day pay and other inducement pay alongside a base salary.

Sample prompts

Copy one into a chat once the connector is on.

  • Use JockTaxes to estimate the after-tax pay for a player on the Florida Panthers with an $8,000,000 salary. Filing status is single.

  • Use JockTaxes to calculate what salary on the New York Rangers nets the same after tax as $7,000,000 on the Tampa Bay Lightning.

  • Use JockTaxes to show how after-tax pay changes if a player moves from the Los Angeles Dodgers to the Texas Rangers on a $20,000,000 salary.

  • Use JockTaxes to analyze potential trade locations for Dylan Larkin and the effects of each destination on his after-tax salary. Use Vegas, Florida, MN, TB and Dallas as comparisons.

  • Use JockTaxes to estimate take-home pay for a Toronto Maple Leafs player earning $5,000,000, including payroll taxes, a 3% agent fee, and an RCA contribution.

Troubleshooting

"Free calculation quota is exhausted"

You've used your allowance, or the shared trial ran out. Sign in with your Jock Taxes account, or create one, using the link the assistant gives you. Then ask again. Upgrade to Pro for 10,000 queries a month.

The assistant asks you to sign in

Follow its link to the Jock Taxes sign-in page and use your email and password. In Claude, if you chose "No sign-in" when you added the connector, remove it and add it again with "Sign in now".

"The connector uses the wrong endpoint"

Each assistant has its own URL. Edit the connector settings and paste the URL the message gives you: /mcp/claude for Claude, /mcp/chatgpt for ChatGPT, /mcp/grok for Grok.

The assistant doesn't use the connector

Start your message with "Use JockTaxes…". In ChatGPT, you can also type @ and pick Jock Taxes. Make sure the connector is turned on for the chat.

ChatGPT shows an internal or script error

This usually means the free allowance is used up. Sign in with your Jock Taxes account and run the same request again.
